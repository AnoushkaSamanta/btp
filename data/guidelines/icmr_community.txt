ICMR / public-health aligned community protocols for ASHA home visits (summarized).

Fever in pregnancy: Consider malaria in endemic areas, UTI, and other infections. High fever is a danger sign. Do not delay referral. Use RDT where trained and available; follow state malaria protocol.

Tuberculosis: Cough more than 2 weeks, night sweats, weight loss — screen and refer to NTEP. Pregnancy does not exclude evaluation.

Immunization in pregnancy: Td as per national schedule. First dose at first contact if unimmunized; second dose 4 weeks later. Booster if previously fully immunized.

Newborn danger signs: poor feeding, lethargy, cold or very hot to touch, fast breathing, chest in-drawing, jaundice on day one or palms/soles, umbilical redness, convulsions. Refer immediately. Counsel skin-to-skin and exclusive breastfeeding.

Postnatal visits: Visit within 24 hours of delivery, day 3, day 7, and around 6 weeks. Check bleeding, fever, BP, perineum, breastfeeding, newborn weight and jaundice, family planning.

Nutrition: Diversified diet, extra meal in pregnancy, iron-rich foods, iodized salt, avoid alcohol and tobacco. Link to Anganwadi THR and PMMVY where eligible.

Documentation: Record vitals, symptoms, counseling given, IFA given, referral, and follow-up date. Worker remains the decision-maker; this tool only supports recall and documentation.
